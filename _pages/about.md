I'm a PhD candidate at the Complexity Science Hub in Vienna, where I develop machine learning methods to study the dynamics of supply chain networks. My broader interests include generative modelling in geometric deep learning, complex adaptive systems, industrial policy, and economic history.

I have a background in physics and mathematics. Earlier work focused on predictive coding and the Bayesian Brain hypothesis.
